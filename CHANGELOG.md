# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-16

First version.

- Kanban board over amazon.com Your Orders, one card per shipment.
- Columns for each expected arrival date, arriving tomorrow and today, late, delivered (today, yesterday, this week, earlier), returns, refunds, cancelled, and in-store orders.
- Loads every page in the selected time range.
- Search, collapsible columns, light and dark themes, and an "Open by default" setting.
